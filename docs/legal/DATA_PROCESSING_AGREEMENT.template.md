## 1. Subject matter & roles
[VENDOR NAME] processes personal data on behalf of the School solely to provide
the NextGen Fusion School software. The School is the controller; [VENDOR NAME]
is the processor.

## 2. Scope of processing
- **Categories of data subjects:** students (minors), guardians, staff.
- **Categories of data:** identity, contact, academic, attendance, financial,
  and employment records (see the Privacy Policy).
- **Duration:** for the term of the license.

## 3. Processor obligations
[VENDOR NAME] shall:
1. Process personal data **only on the School's documented instructions**.
2. Ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality.
3. Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
   (access control, encryption in transit, audit logging, backups).
4. Not engage sub-processors without the School's prior authorization; list any
   at **[SUB-PROCESSORS, e.g. Resend (email), ImageKit (media), hosting]**.
5. Assist the School with data-subject requests (access, correction, erasure,
   portability) using the built-in export/erasure tools.
6. Notify the School **without undue delay** (and in any case within **[N]**
   hours) of a personal-data breach.
7. On termination, **delete or return** all personal data at the School's
   choice, subject to legal retention.

## 4. Self-hosted deployments
Where the School hosts the software on its own infrastructure, the School
controls the database and backups; [VENDOR NAME]'s processing is limited to
support access expressly requested by the School, and to any sub-processors
listed above (e.g. email/media). Support access is logged.
